`UnsupportedOperationException` with Folia means you are not using it in the right scheduler.&#xD;&#xA;&#xD;&#xA;On Folia, lot of actions should be run on specific scheduler. The use case is:&#xD;&#xA;- [`GlobalRegionScheduler`](https://jd.papermc.io/folia/1.20/io/papermc/paper/threadedregions/scheduler/GlobalRegionScheduler.html) for all world-related content but not about a specific region&#xD;&#xA;- [`RegionScheduler`](https://jd.papermc.io/folia/1.20/io/papermc/paper/threadedregions/scheduler/RegionScheduler.html) for everything related to a specific part of the world (a block, a chunk etc)&#xD;&#xA;- [`EntityScheduler`](https://jd.papermc.io/folia/1.20/io/papermc/paper/threadedregions/scheduler/EntityScheduler.html) for everything which is entity-related&#xD;&#xA;- [`AsyncScheduler`](https://jd.papermc.io/folia/1.20/io/papermc/paper/threadedregions/scheduler/AsyncScheduler.html) for all async tasks&#xD;&#xA;&#xD;&#xA;In your case, you want to load/save the world. It's world-related (read the world content), but not about a specific region. So, you should use `GlobalRegionScheduler`. You can get it thanks to [`Bukkit.getGlobalRegionScheduler()`](https://jd.papermc.io/folia/1.20/org/bukkit/Bukkit.html#getGlobalRegionScheduler())&#xD;&#xA;&#xD;&#xA;If, for example, you want to do async tasks, you can firstly do an async task, then only when you need, "go" on the global region scheduler to do what you want.&#xD;&#xA;&#xD;&#xA;Note: In your specific case, the `Bukkit.createWorld(WorldCreator);` method seems not allowed on folia. You should use others `Bukkit.createWorld(String name, Environment env etc)`, or simply use `worldCreator.createWorld()`.
